01

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide you with the AI Landing Page, publishing, and hosting services. This includes:

Account Information

Name, email address, password hash, and OAuth credentials (when signing in with Google or Apple).

Website Content & Media

Site configurations, page layouts, text copy, images, uploaded branding assets, and custom domain DNS records.

AI Interactions

Prompts, edit instructions, and generation history sent to our AI pipeline to draft and remix website sections.

Technical & Device Data

IP address, operating system, browser type, and diagnostic logs to ensure high availability and prevent abuse.

02

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used to deliver, maintain, and enhance the AI Landing Page platform:

  • Compiling and rendering your website HTML, CSS, and modern responsive components.
  • Managing live deployments, edge CDN hosting, and SSL certificates.
  • Processing subscription billing, invoices, and entitlements via Stripe.
  • Restoring revisions, draft history, and synchronizing state across your devices.
03

3. AI Generation & Privacy Safeguards

We respect your creative work and intellectual property:

Zero Training on User Data

Your website copy, prompts, private drafts, and uploaded media are never used to train public AI foundation models.

Isolated Context

Prompts passed to OpenRouter and our LLM endpoints exist solely for the duration of the generation request.

Human Oversight

You retain full editorial control to review, accept, reject, or undo any AI-generated layout.

04

4. Storage & Security

All data is protected using industry-standard security measures:

Encryption in Transit

All API traffic, file transfers, and editing sessions are encrypted using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.

Encryption at Rest

User records, site documents, and media assets are stored in encrypted databases and secure cloud buckets.

Access Controls

Principle of least privilege is enforced for all infrastructure access.

05

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with vetted, secure infrastructure providers to operate the platform:

Cloudflare & Edge

For global DNS routing, DDoS protection, and SSL termination.

Stripe

For PCI-compliant subscription payment processing.

Cloud Asset Storage

For serving high-speed responsive imagery and uploads.

06

6. Your Rights & Data Portability

You maintain total ownership of your websites and uploaded media:

Export Your Code

Export your sites into standalone static HTML/CSS/JS at any time.

Delete Your Data

You may delete individual websites, pages, uploaded images, or request account deletion at any time via Edit Profile.

Access & Rectification

Update your profile name, email, or language preferences directly from the Profile settings.
